I am RaIN. Born in Hong Kong, 1983. By day I run buildings — a Building Manager with an IT background. By night, and most weekends, I make iOS apps and small tools using a workflow I call Vibe Code, with Claude Code as my pair.

I am not a full-time developer. I'm the person who makes things work. Debugging, automation, infrastructure, document craft — anything that turns a half-formed idea into something you can show, share, or ship.

What I value most is the kind of output you do not have to fix before you send it on. Complete, composed, and quietly insightful. Anything less is half a sentence.
